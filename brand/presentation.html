What we heard

The brief

Reactive Forge extracts React components into portable metadata, lets people compose them visually and exports the result to TSX. The mark should feel precise, structural and crafted, and avoid the React atom, </> brackets and flames.

precisestructuralcrafteddeveloper-first

Success: reads as an anvil at 16 px, carries a quiet JSX hint, works in one colour and reversed.
